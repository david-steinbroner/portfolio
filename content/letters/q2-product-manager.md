I'm a senior product manager who turns custom, bank-by-bank fintech work into products that scale. That's the harder half of what this role is really asking for. The posting says account opening and onboarding, but I can see that the real mandate is taking bespoke workflows, partner integrations, back-office controls, user management, and compliance, and turning all of it into a product suite that feels simple to the people using it. I did exactly that at Fold, a fintech I was employee 11 at and helped take to the Nasdaq last year.

For most of my time there, I was effectively the primary PM across the financial ecosystem: account onboarding, KYC flows, banking rails, crypto features, rewards, vendor integrations, partner approvals, and tax compliance. Because we stayed a small team right up until we went public, I led tight-knit efforts under real regulatory constraints and high stakes while moving fast.

One example: evolving a stack of separate features into one easy-to-use product that drove major signups and volume. I inherited a closed-loop prepaid card where users funded the card, earned rewards, and redeemed in-app, and helped extend it into a [full financial platform](/case-studies/fiat-bitcoin-ecosystem): checking-account rebrand, ACH, direct deposit, bill pay, and bitcoin buy/sell with auto-buy on deposit. That feature set drove a roughly 50% trade-volume increase and 40% auto-buy adoption.

From there, we made the repeatable parts scalable. I reworked the [rewards economy](/case-studies/spin-wheel) from an unsustainable model into a scalable one, with self-service admin tooling and a six-figure sponsorship line. I cut external [banking-partner approval](/features/banking-partner-approval) from 4+ weeks to days. And I led the [tax-reporting integration](/case-studies/taxbit) from vendor sourcing to launch 2.5 months early, with sign-off across 6 stakeholder groups, 90% adoption, and a 2% ticket rate.

The account-opening work was direct. I led the [onboarding and account-creation flow redesigns](/case-studies/onboarding) around Persona, Unit21, and Sardine, and built the KYC-review operation with Compliance and Support: automated comms when a user had to submit documents, review queues, and funnels showing who was stuck and how signup-to-active turnaround was moving. I've run account opening as a real operating system, not a static form, and maintained state-by-state eligibility as custodians gained or lost licenses.

Q2 needs a PM who can sit between business strategy, user needs, and technical execution and turn custom solutions into future-proofed products fast. I've done that inside a highly regulated, complex fintech ecosystem, and I'd love to do it again for Q2.
