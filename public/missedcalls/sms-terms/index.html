Missed-Call Rescue · Effective September 14, 2026

What this is

Missed-Call Rescue is a service run by David Steinbroner in Austin, TX. When you call a business that uses it, or our demo line at (737) 344-5601, and nobody can pick up, you hear a short recorded message telling you that you'll get a text at the number you called from. By calling and hearing that message, you agree to receive text messages about your call.

What we text you

  • A text asking what you need and how soon you need it.
  • A short confirmation after you reply, so you know your message reached the business.

These texts are only about the call you made. We don't send marketing or promotional texts.

How often

Usually 1 to 3 messages for each missed call. Message frequency varies with how many calls you make and how you reply.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You'll get one message confirming you've opted out, and no more after that. Reply HELP for help, or email davidsteinbroner@gmail.com.

Delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

How we handle your number and messages is covered in our Privacy Policy. We never share your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with third parties for their marketing.
